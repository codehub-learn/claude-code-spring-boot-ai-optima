# Shipping and delivery

Orders placed before 15:00 on a working day ship the same day from the Athens warehouse.

- **Standard delivery**: 1-2 working days in Attica and Thessaloniki, 2-4 working days elsewhere in Greece. Free over 50 EUR,
  otherwise 3.90 EUR.
- **Express delivery**: next working day before 12:00, in Attica and Thessaloniki only, 9.90 EUR.
- **Islands**: add 1-2 working days; express is not available.
- **Store pickup**: free, from the Athens or Thessaloniki store, ready within 2 hours during opening hours.
- **Other EU countries**: 3-6 working days, 14.90 EUR, free over 300 EUR.

Laptops, smartphones and orders over 500 EUR need a signature on delivery. Every order gets a tracking link by email as soon as it
leaves the warehouse.

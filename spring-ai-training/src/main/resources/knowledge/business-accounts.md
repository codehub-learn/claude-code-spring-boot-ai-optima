# Business accounts

A business account lets companies, public organisations and self-employed professionals buy with VAT invoices, credit terms and
volume pricing, and manage purchases for a team. Opening an account is free and takes about one working day.

## Who can open one

Any business registered in the European Union with a valid VAT number can open a business account. Self-employed professionals
qualify too. Schools, universities and public bodies can open an account without a VAT number by uploading their founding
document instead.

To open an account, fill in the form on the business page with the company name, VAT number, registered address and a contact
person. We check the VAT number against the EU VIES register and email you when the account is active, usually within one working
day.

## Users and roles

One business account can have up to 50 users. Each user has one of three roles:

- **Administrator**: manages users, addresses and payment methods, and sees every order.
- **Buyer**: places orders, which may need approval.
- **Approver**: approves or rejects orders placed by buyers.

Administrators can set an approval limit per buyer: orders above the limit wait for an approver before they are processed. An
order waiting for approval reserves stock for 48 hours.

## Invoices and VAT

Every order gets an invoice with the company's VAT number, available as a PDF from the order page and sent by email to the
administrator. Invoices are also available through our e-invoicing connection for companies registered with the Greek myDATA
platform.

Businesses in other EU countries with a valid VAT number are invoiced without Greek VAT, under the reverse charge rule. The VAT
number is re-checked on every order; if it is no longer valid, the order is invoiced with Greek VAT and the administrator is
notified.

## Payment on credit

After 3 months of trading and at least 3 paid orders, a business can apply for payment on credit. Approved accounts pay by bank
transfer within 30 days of the invoice date, up to a credit limit that is set after a credit check, usually between 5,000 and
50,000 EUR.

Late payments are charged interest at the statutory rate. If an invoice is more than 15 days overdue, new orders are held until it
is paid.

Accounts without credit pay by card, bank transfer, or direct debit from a SEPA business account.

## Volume pricing

Business accounts see business prices, which are lower than consumer prices on most laptops, monitors and accessories. For larger
purchases, discounts increase with the number of units of the same product in one order:

| Units of the same product | Extra discount |
|---------------------------|----------------|
| 10 to 49                  | 3%             |
| 50 to 199                 | 5%             |
| 200 or more               | ask for a quote |

For rollouts of more than 200 devices, the business team prepares a custom quote with fixed prices for up to 6 months, and can
stage deliveries in several batches.

## Deployment services

For laptops and phones, we can prepare devices before delivery, for a fee per device: asset tagging, installing the company's
operating system image, and enrolling the devices in the company's device management system, such as Microsoft Intune or Jamf.
Enrolled devices arrive ready to use: the employee signs in and the company's settings and apps install automatically.

Deployment adds 3 to 5 working days to the delivery time, depending on the number of devices.

## Business warranty and support

Business customers get the standard warranty plus a priority support line with a response within 4 working hours. For laptops,
the Business Care option adds next-business-day on-site repair anywhere in mainland Greece for the warranty period. Laptops with
Business Care also get a replacement battery once, at any time during the warranty.

Business returns follow the returns policy, except that the 14-day legal right of withdrawal applies only to consumers: business
orders can be returned only if unopened, within 30 days.

## Closing an account

An administrator can close the account at any time from the account settings, once every invoice is paid. Order history and
invoices remain available for download for 10 years, as Greek tax law requires.

# Laptop buying guide

**Office work, travel.** A light 13-14" laptop with 16 GB RAM is enough for email, documents, video calls and many browser tabs.
Look at the Latitude 14 Ultralight: long battery life and 1.2 kg.

**Drawing and note taking.** A 2-in-1 convertible folds into a tablet and takes a pen. The Flex 13 Convertible supports a pen
out of the box.

**Photo and video editing.** Colour accuracy and a fast SSD matter more than raw CPU speed. The Nomad 15 Creator has a 4K OLED
screen covering the full DCI-P3 colour space and a 1 TB SSD.

**3D, CAD, machine learning, gaming.** You need a discrete GPU and at least 32 GB RAM. The Latitude 16 Workstation is the only
laptop in the range with both.

**How much RAM?** 16 GB for everyday use; 32 GB for virtual machines, large code bases, or editing 4K video. RAM is soldered on
all current models and cannot be upgraded later, so buy what you will need in three years.

**Storage.** 512 GB is tight once photos and projects pile up; add a Voyager Portable SSD for backups and archives.

# Warranty

Every product carries the manufacturer's warranty, handled by the store so you have one place to go.

| Category                     | Warranty                                                    |
|------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Laptops                      | 2 years (batteries 1 year)                                  |
| Smartphones                  | 2 years (batteries 1 year)                                  |
| Monitors                     | 3 years, including dead-pixel cover in the first 6 months   |
| Audio                        | 1 year                                                      |
| Storage (SSDs, memory cards) | 5 years or the rated write endurance, whichever comes first |
| Accessories                  | 1 year                                                      |

The warranty covers manufacturing faults. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or wear such as scratched cases.
Laptops and smartphones can get **Care+** at purchase: 3 extra years and one accidental damage repair, for 12% of the price.

To claim, open a support ticket with the order number and a description of the fault. Repairs take 7 to 10 working days; if
the same fault comes back twice, the product is replaced.

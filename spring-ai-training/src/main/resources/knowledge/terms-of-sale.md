# Terms of sale

These terms apply to every order placed with the store, online or in person. They sit alongside the returns policy, the warranty
terms and the shipping page, which cover those topics in more detail. Where this document and a product page disagree, the
product page wins for that product only.

## Who we sell to

We sell to consumers aged 18 or over and to registered businesses. An order placed by someone under 18 is cancelled and refunded
in full as soon as we notice.

Business customers who want invoices with a VAT number, credit terms or volume pricing should open a business account; the
business accounts document explains how. Orders placed without a business account are treated as consumer orders, even when a
company name is given in the address.

## Placing an order

An order is an offer to buy. The contract is formed only when we send the dispatch confirmation email, not when you receive the
automatic order acknowledgement. Until dispatch we may decline an order, for example when a product turns out to be out of stock
or when the payment check fails.

You can change the delivery address or the delivery option for free until the order is packed, usually within two hours of
ordering. After that, changes are only possible by cancelling the order and placing a new one.

A single order can contain at most 5 units of the same product. Larger quantities are sold only through a business account.

## Prices and pricing errors

All prices are in euro and include Greek VAT at 24%. Delivery costs are shown separately at checkout before you pay.

Prices can change at any time, but the price that applies is the one shown when you placed the order. If a product drops in
price within 14 days after you received it, we refund the difference once, as store credit, on request.

If we discover an obvious pricing error, such as a laptop listed at one tenth of its usual price, we will contact you before
dispatch and offer the correct price or a full cancellation. We will not ship at the wrong price and then ask you to pay more.

## Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, PayPal, and bank transfer. Cash on delivery is
available for orders up to 500 EUR, with a fee of 2 EUR.

Card payments are authorised when you order and charged when we dispatch. If an order is split into several parcels, each
parcel is charged when it leaves the warehouse.

Bank transfer orders are held for 3 working days. If the payment has not arrived by then, the order is cancelled automatically
and the reserved stock is released.

### Paying in instalments

Orders over 300 EUR can be paid in up to 24 monthly instalments with a credit card issued by a Greek bank. Up to 12 instalments
are interest-free; from 13 to 24 the card issuer's interest applies. The number of instalments is chosen at checkout and cannot
be changed afterwards.

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order online until it is dispatched, and we refund the full amount within 5 working days.

After dispatch you cannot cancel, but you can still use your legal right of withdrawal: 14 days from delivery to tell us you
want to withdraw, and another 14 days to send the product back. The returns policy explains what state the product must be in
and when a restocking fee applies.

Pre-orders can be cancelled at any time before the release date without any charge.

## Pre-orders and back orders

Pre-order products are charged on the release date, not when you place the pre-order. If the release date moves by more than 30
days, we email you and you can cancel with one click.

Back-ordered products, meaning products that are temporarily out of stock, are shown with an expected date. If we cannot deliver
within 30 days of that date, the order is cancelled and refunded automatically.

## Delivery and risk

Delivery times and costs are on the shipping page. The risk of loss or damage passes to you when you, or someone you have named,
receives the parcel.

Check the parcel in front of the courier. If the box is visibly damaged, write that on the courier's receipt or refuse the
parcel; we then send a replacement at no cost. Damage that only shows once the box is opened must be reported within 48 hours,
with photos of the box and the product.

## Gift cards and store credit

Gift cards are valid for 2 years from purchase and store credit for 1 year from the day it is issued. Neither can be exchanged
for cash, and a lost gift card cannot be replaced unless it was registered to an account.

Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards.

## Personal data

We use your personal data only to process your order, provide support and meet our legal obligations. We never sell it.
Marketing emails are sent only if you opt in, and every email contains an unsubscribe link. You can ask for a copy of your data
or for its deletion from your account settings; we answer within 30 days.

## Complaints and disputes

Send complaints to the support team from your order page. We reply within 2 working days and aim to resolve every complaint
within 15 working days.

If we cannot agree, you can turn to the Consumer Ombudsman or use the European Commission's online dispute resolution platform.
These terms are governed by Greek law, and the courts of Athens have jurisdiction, without taking away any right you have as a
consumer to go to the courts where you live.

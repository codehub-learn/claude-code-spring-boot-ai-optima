# Returns policy

You can return any product bought from the store within 30 days of delivery.

- **Unopened products** (factory seal intact) get a full refund to the original payment method, usually within 5 working days
  of the return arriving at the warehouse.
- **Opened products** in full working order, with all accessories and the original box, get store credit for the full price.
  If you prefer a refund to your card, a 10% restocking fee applies.
- **Headphones, earbuds and in-ear monitors** can only be returned unopened, for hygiene reasons. Faulty ones are covered by the
  warranty instead.
- **Memory cards and SSDs** that have been used can be returned only if faulty, because they may hold personal data.

Return shipping is free for faulty products and for orders over 100 EUR; otherwise it costs 6.90 EUR, deducted from the refund.
Start a return from your order page; you will get a prepaid label by email.

# Trade-in and recycling

You can trade in an old device for store credit when you buy a new one, and recycle any electronic device with us for free, even
if you did not buy it here. This document explains which devices qualify, how they are valued, and what happens to your data.

## Which devices qualify for trade-in

We accept smartphones, laptops, tablets and smartwatches from the last 6 years, from any brand. Monitors, headphones, speakers and
accessories are not accepted for trade-in, but they can be recycled.

A device qualifies only if it turns on, is not reported lost or stolen, and is signed out of every account: iCloud, Google, Samsung
or Microsoft. A device that is still locked to an account is returned to you at our cost, or recycled if you prefer.

## How a device is valued

The online quote is based on the model, the storage and the condition you describe. Condition has four grades:

- **Like new**: no visible marks, battery health above 85%.
- **Good**: light scratches on the case, no marks on the screen.
- **Fair**: visible scratches or dents, or a screen with marks that do not affect use.
- **Faulty**: cracked screen, broken buttons, a battery that swells, or any fault that stops normal use.

Faulty devices are still accepted, at a lower value. Water-damaged devices are graded faulty.

When the device arrives, a technician checks it within 3 working days. If the condition matches your description, you get the
quoted value. If it does not, we send a revised quote, which you can accept or refuse. If you refuse, we return the device for
free.

## How you get paid

Trade-in value is paid as store credit, valid for 1 year, not as cash. When you trade in during checkout of a new purchase, the
quote is deducted from the price straight away and the credit is secured by a card authorisation, which is released once the old
device passes the check.

During trade-in campaigns, usually around new phone launches, we add a bonus of 10% to 20% on top of the quote for selected new
products. The bonus applies only when the trade-in and the purchase are in the same order.

## Sending the device

After you accept a quote, you receive a prepaid, insured shipping label by email, valid for 14 days. Pack the device in a padded
envelope or a box; you do not need to include the charger or the original box. You can also hand the device in at the Athens or
Thessaloniki store, where a technician can check it while you wait and credit you immediately.

## Your data

Before you send a device, back up your data and reset it to factory settings. Every device we receive is wiped again with
certified data erasure software that meets the NIST 800-88 standard, whether it is resold or recycled. For devices that cannot be
wiped because they do not turn on, the storage chip is physically shredded.

You receive an erasure certificate by email for every device, with its serial number. We never access the data on a device.

## Recycling

We recycle any electronic device, cable or battery for free, in any condition, from any brand, whether or not you bought it here.
Drop it in the recycling bins at either store, or add it to a delivery: the courier takes up to 3 small devices back when they
deliver an order, if you tick the recycling box at checkout.

Large items, such as monitors over 27 inches, are collected for free when we deliver a new product of the same kind.

Swollen or damaged batteries must not be sent by post, because they can catch fire. Tape the terminals, put the battery in a
plastic bag, and bring it to a store, where it is stored in a fire-proof container.

## What happens to recycled devices

Devices in working order are refurbished and resold, or donated to schools through our education programme. Devices that cannot
be repaired are dismantled by a certified recycling partner, which recovers metals such as copper, gold and cobalt. Last year
92% of the weight of recycled devices was recovered as material.
